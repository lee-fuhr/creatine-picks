Creatine monohydrate is one of the most researched and effective supplements for building strength, power, and lean mass. The good news is that the cheapest forms, unflavored, micronized powder, work just as well as pricey blends. To find the best budget creatine, you need to look beyond the price tag: check particle size (micronized mixes easier), third-party testing reputation, and cost per serving. A 500-gram tub typically gives you 100 servings at a standard 5-gram dose, so a few dollars difference per container adds up over time. All three picks here are unflavored, pure monohydrate powders with no fillers or artificial sweeteners. They differ mainly in tub size, brand transparency, and how finely the powder is milled. I’ve leaned on expert reviews, long-term user feedback, and ingredient-label scrutiny to rank them, not lab tests or personal trials.
