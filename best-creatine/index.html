Creatine monohydrate is one of the most researched and proven supplements for strength, power, and muscle recovery. It works by helping your muscles produce more energy during high-intensity exercise, and countless studies support its safety and effectiveness. The key is choosing a brand that uses high-quality raw materials, preferably micronized for better mixing and absorption, and that has transparent sourcing and third-party testing. When you’re shopping for creatine, the main variables are purity (look for Creapure® or similarly certified sources), particle size (micronized dissolves more easily), and serving size. You don’t need fancy blends or flavors, plain, unflavored powder is the most versatile and cost-effective form.
